About

SNU's Qiskit Fall Fest, built by SQRT

SQRT (SNU Quantum Research Team) is a student academic society under Seoul National University's College of Natural Sciences club association, founded in fall 2022, with about 140 active members. Students from physics and computer science, along with many other backgrounds, run study groups on quantum information theory, linear algebra, and quantum mechanics, and take on student-led projects. The team has competed in Xanadu QHack, the IBM Open Science Prize, and the IBM Quantum Challenge. SQRT is supported by SNU's Hybrid Quantum Computing Center (IRC) and is a member club of QISCA, the Korean university quantum society, maintaining ties with the domestic student quantum community.

What is Qiskit Fall Fest

A global quantum computing celebration

Qiskit Fall Fest is IBM Quantum's global series of events, planned and run independently by host teams around the world. In 2025, over 32,000 participants joined events led by 150 hosts. The 2026 theme, "a decade of quantum on the cloud," marks ten years since IBM first opened a quantum computer to the cloud.

At SNU, SQRT is hosting the event, preparing a 3-day program of a seminar, a two-track hackathon, and a poster session, with support from IBM Quantum and SNU's Hybrid Quantum Computing Center (IRC). It's open to any student interested in quantum information or computing at a domestic university, not just SNU students.

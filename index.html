About Qiskit Fall Fest

Quantum, learned
together.

Qiskit Fall Fest brings the global quantum community together to learn and experiment with quantum computing. Hosted virtually At, ZAG-ENG this event connects foundational concepts, hands-on Qiskit coding, and real-world engineering use cases—helping curiosity become a next step.

  • Start with concepts and tools, even if quantum computing is new to you.
  • Build and execute real quantum circuits using Qiskit alongside an IBM guest speaker.
  • Discover engineering applications and pathways into the global IBM Quantum community.

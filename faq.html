FAQ

Have a question?

I'm not a physics or CS major, can I still join?

Yes, absolutely. Ideathon is designed so you can join with zero coding or physics background. Econ, business, humanities, design, any major is welcome.

Is track eligibility limited by major?

No. Neither track is restricted by major. Hackathon is based on coding or Qiskit experience, not your field of study, and Ideathon is built for first-timers. Note that each participant may join only one track (no double-registering across both).

Is this only open to SNU students?

No. Any student at a domestic (Korean) university interested in quantum information or computing can join, regardless of school.

Do I need to know Qiskit beforehand?

It depends on the track. Hackathon is aimed at participants with some Qiskit or coding experience. Ideathon requires no prior knowledge.

Can I join a hackathon track and the Poster Session at the same time?

Not yet decided (TBD). We'll update this page as soon as it's confirmed.

Do I need a team, or can I join solo?

Team rules aren't finalized yet (TBD). We'll announce solo/team policy for both tracks once it's decided.

Can I attend the Day 1 seminar without registering for a hackathon track?

Yes. The Day 1 seminar is planned as an open, free RSVP that anyone can attend.

What language is the event held in?

The seminar and Ideathon run primarily in Korean. Hackathon's problem materials will likely be in English, following common IBM-judged event practice (not yet confirmed).

Is there a participation fee?

We expect it to be free, but this isn't finalized yet (TBD).

What does an Ideathon submission look like?

We expect an idea memo or a short slide deck. The exact format will be posted on the Register page (TBD).

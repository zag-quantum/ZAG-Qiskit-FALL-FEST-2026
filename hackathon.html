BEGINNER

Ideathon

No coding. No physics. No prerequisites.

Built for students meeting quantum computing for the first time, especially those from econ, business, humanities, or design. The goal isn't writing finished code. It's asking a good question.

Register for Ideathon

The kind of ideas we're looking for

  • Econ / finance: Where could a quantum algorithm help with portfolio optimization or risk modeling?
  • Business / strategy: What could quantum computing mean for your industry in the next decade?
  • Humanities: What new questions does quantum computing raise about knowledge, language, or ethics?
  • Design / art: Could quantum randomness or algorithms become a creative tool?

How it works

Pick up the prompt at the Day 2 briefing, then develop your idea solo or in a team over the take-home window. Like Hackathon, you'll get a short weekend presentation slot. Presentation format is TBD.
